---
description: When revising operational runbooks, state prerequisites, expected observations, recovery steps and actual validation evidence.
applyTo: 'docs/runbooks/**/*.md'
---

For each procedure, record required access and inputs, numbered actions, expected
observable results, and recovery or escalation steps. Preserve author-supplied
commands; leave missing service names, URLs and contacts as explicit placeholders.

Separate expected output from observed validation. State what was checked and the
supplied result; when no execution evidence exists, write "Not executed". Editing
a runbook does not authorize executing its procedure. Finish with unresolved
inputs and the next check the runbook owner should perform.
