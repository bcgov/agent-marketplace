Frequently Asked Questions

What is published here?

Agent Skills only: portable SKILL.md instructions plus optional flat resources and closed marketplace metadata. Hooks, MCP servers, custom agents, and automatically executable extension types are not supported by this MVP.

Does “Marketplace reviewed” mean safe?

No. It means named reviewers assessed the exact published revision, digest, declared capabilities, and available evidence. The marketplace does not use “safe” or “verified secure” claims.

What does “Unreviewed candidate” mean?

An immutable third-party source was submitted for review. Trusted CI still needs to compute its digest and run deterministic policy checks, and BC Gov reviewers have not approved it.

Why is the source a full commit SHA?

A branch or tag can move. A full commit SHA plus package-tree digest binds discovery, scan, review, install, and receipt evidence to the same bytes.

Why are there two companion skills?

bcgov-find-extension handles discovery, controlled installation, and third-party candidate registration. bcgov-create-extension handles authoring and hosted submission. Keeping the entry points separate loads only the workflow needed for the request.

Are installed skills global too?

Reviewed catalog skills default to the current project. Third-party candidates are project-local only. The finder shows the exact destination and material capabilities before confirmation.

Where is installation evidence?

Preferred APM installs use apm.yml and apm.lock.yaml; the fallback installer may write skills-lock.json. Legacy helper receipts live under .github/agent-marketplace/receipts/. Local evidence stays in the project unless the developer chooses to share it for support or incident response.

Can a companion publish its own work?

No. The creator may open a signed draft pull request, but cannot approve, merge, modify protection, or assign review status. CI and human reviewers decide publication.

What if I cannot push a branch?

The creator preserves the staged package and provides an access-request action. Fork PRs are intentionally blocked. Third-party registration uses issues and does not require branch access.

How is a package quarantined?

Maintainers set its lifecycle to quarantined, regenerate, and publish through review. The finder excludes it from default results. Support then identifies affected receipts or declared dependencies and publishes update or removal guidance.
