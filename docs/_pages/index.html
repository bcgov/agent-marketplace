Trust is attached to bytes

Every record shows how far its exact revision and digest were checked. Editing a package drops it back to the automated tier until a human signs off again.

Unreviewed candidate

An immutable third-party source awaiting trusted CI scanning and human review. Not shown in the catalogue.

Auto-checked

Community packages whose exact bytes passed automated policy and content scanning. No human review is claimed.

Domain reviewed

A specialty owner, such as security, reviewed the exact revision and digest.

Marketplace reviewed

Named reviewers assessed the exact published commit, package digest, and declared capabilities.

Marketplace boundary

The MVP publishes skills, prompts, instructions, and agent definitions. Hooks, MCP servers, and silent automatic execution are out of scope. Installation or import always identifies a destination and requires confirmation.
