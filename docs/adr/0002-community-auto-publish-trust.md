# Community auto-publish trusts hard gates, not review bypass

[Decisions #12](https://github.com/bcgov/agent-marketplace/issues/12), [#13](https://github.com/bcgov/agent-marketplace/issues/13), [#29](https://github.com/bcgov/agent-marketplace/issues/29) and the [locked v1 amendments](https://github.com/bcgov/agent-marketplace/issues/7) choose a trusted-main API-only merge gate for eligible Community changes instead of requiring human review of every ordinary submission: public bcgov membership, trusted-base maintainer eligibility, approved deterministic blockers, bounded risk and rolling new-identity quota constrain that trust. Intended layered rulesets let supported Actions bypass only human review, while strict checks/current base, source signatures and CodeQL remain no-bypass; app-level authority therefore also trusts limited repository writers and reviewed workflow changes. [Activation #43](https://github.com/bcgov/agent-marketplace/issues/43) must prove actor feasibility and positive/negative fork behavior before either independent automatic route is enabled, with no broader credential fallback; [historical research](../research/fork-pr-automerge.md) preserves rejected earlier approaches.
